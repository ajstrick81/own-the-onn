# AML-S0003 — coffey read-only baseline adb capture

## What it is
The read-only adb collection performed under experiment `AML-E0001` on
2026-10-08 (adb 1.0.41, device `onn/coffey/coffey:14/URO4.260304.011.B1/
15051976:user/release-keys`). getprop, `/proc/config.gz` (preserved compressed,
hashed before decompression), `/proc/version`, binary Device-Tree reads,
`/proc/cpuinfo`, and read-only `/dev/block` + interface listings. Raw captures
and the sha256 manifest are held **off-repo** (gitignored scratch), privacy
reviewed; only field-level technical facts are recorded here.

## What it establishes
Device-reported identity, kernel, SoC, partition/boot architecture, and
diagnostic-interface facts for coffey — see findings `AML-F0001` (identity),
`AML-F0002` (partitions/boot), `AML-F0003` (interfaces). Device-**reported**
(getprop/DT/uname), not an independent hardware measurement.

## Access limits observed (recorded, not escalated)
`/proc/partitions`, `/proc/cmdline`, `/proc/device-tree/compatible` →
permission denied; `/proc/device-tree/model` → not present. No privileged
operation was introduced to obtain these; they remain unresolved.

## Credibility note
`primary` — direct read from the device's own build properties and kernel
interfaces over adb, on owner hardware, read-only. Raw dumps are not published;
identifiers (serial, MACs, accounts, vbmeta digest) were kept out of this record
